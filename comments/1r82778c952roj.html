Gameplay is about farming and seems to be a copy of popular farming simulation games both on PC and mobile. The graphics are coordinated no doubt, and the UI is actually one of the stronger parts. However, I don’t see very strongly how this is accounting.

Perhaps, you can sell the game as more of an accounting farm game. For example, depreciation of farming equipment, crop inventory valuation, cost of seeds and fertilizer, revenue from harvest, and budgeting for the farm can all become gameplay mechanics. These are accounting concepts, but they are not explained clearly in the current presentation.

There is no effort to flesh out the differences between this and common farming games. The team presented a filtered table with data, but no visualization was used. I do appreciate that a lot of the UI is very coordinated, but the linkage is a little weak. Overall, visually good, but accounting connection should be much stronger.
