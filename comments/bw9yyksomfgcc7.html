Aryan, Fiona, Ahmed, Suzie, Maha.

Gameplay is about dishonorable auditing. I do enjoy the gameplay about financial stress and exposure. This seems like it is a balance between good and evil meters for the player, which is quite interesting because it turns ethical pressure into a game mechanic.

The dashboarding is quite well done compared to other groups. The game also has a more direct accounting link because it relates to audit, financial filing, and ethical choices. Overall, the design is simple and decent.

What I would do is to make the theme blend in with the slides to be darker, but this is perhaps not the best use of time. Also, for the player to review financial filings, this would have to pitch the game at very high-level accounting students or practitioners. Would these people even want to play this game? Perhaps the mentioning of guided practice or beginner levels would help. Overall, this is a strong concept, but the audience and level of difficulty should be clearer.
