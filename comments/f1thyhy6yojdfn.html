Gameplay is about forensic auditing, which is something different from the majority of the presented games. I appreciate this because many other groups used common simulation or tycoon game ideas, but this group picked a more accounting-specific concept.

Two bar charts are shown, and DLC is shown but not expanded on. The game is quite wordy, and the dashboard is shown, but the core mechanic, such as the actual task, is not shown clearly enough. I would have liked to see what the player actually clicks, reviews, investigates, or decides during the forensic audit.

I do appreciate the DA that found fraud as the number one theme, and this is linked to the topic. However, the presentation was mundane. Slide design was rather gloomy in color theme. I wish more were focused on the actual forensic auditing gameplay, such as reviewing fake invoices, flagging suspicious transactions, or identifying red flags. Right now it feels too high-level.
