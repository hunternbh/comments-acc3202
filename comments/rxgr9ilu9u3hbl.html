Tony Zeng, Rick, Melody, Thiti, Sezim, Imanol.

I actually liked this group and it was close to being the top group, although it was outvoted by a few points. This is a cooking game with more of a focus on the accounting. The DA was quite uncoordinated but the background graphics were very spot-on. I forgot who was the one who designed them, perhaps Tony or Melody, but it was well-done.

However, I would have preferred the words to be more beefed up. The UI is good but the game risks being another reproduction of a cooking game. More needs to be done about where the link to accounting is.

For example, the game can include spoilage or sales forecasting. These ideas can make the accounting connection more direct. Overall, the group had one of the nicer visual styles, and the game is memorable, but the accounting gameplay should be more clearly explained.
